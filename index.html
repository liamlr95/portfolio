FAQs

Common questions, briefly answered.

Do I need to be a Virgin Active member?
Yes — Academy programmes are an add-on to a Virgin Active membership. Membership and Academy can be joined together at sign-up.
What ages do you coach?
Learn to Swim from age 3, competitive squads from 7+, and adult squads for swimmers 18 and over.
How are squads selected?
A coach runs a free in-pool assessment so we place every swimmer in the right level — never just by age.
Where can I train?
At participating Virgin Active clubs nationwide. Use the club finder above to see your nearest options.
Will my child compete?
Only when they're ready and only if they want to. Competition is built into the squad path but is not required for Learn to Swim or Adult squads.
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on your club and squad. After you book a free assessment, we send a tailored quote with available session times.
Can I cancel or move squads?
Yes. You can pause, cancel, or move squads at any time — coaches re-assess swimmers each term to make sure they're in the right place.
